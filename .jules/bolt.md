## 2024-05-18 - Hoisting ImageData buffers out of render loops
**Learning:** In performance-critical canvas rendering (like WebGL fallbacks, shaders, or 60fps games), `createImageData` allocates a new `Uint8ClampedArray` (pixel buffer) under the hood. Doing this on every tick inside `requestAnimationFrame` or high-frequency render loops rapidly fills memory, causing massive garbage collection (GC) spikes that visibly stutter the application.
**Action:** Always allocate `ImageData` objects and their associated pixel buffers *once* outside the render loop. To clear the buffer before reuse in a new frame, call `.fill(0)` on the underlying data array (`imageData.data.fill(0)`). For React components where the loop is inside a `useEffect` or `useCallback` that gets rebuilt on dependency changes, use `useRef` to maintain a stable reference to the `ImageData` buffer across re-renders.
